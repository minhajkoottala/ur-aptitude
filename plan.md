Open Source Career Guidance Platform: Project Blueprint
1. Core Vision & Strategy
An open-source, highly accurate career and path-finding platform tailored for the Indian demographic.

Need some gamification (fun stress reliefing things)

The Career Directory: A dedicated hub detailing Indian market salaries, educational pathways (like +2 streams and entrance exams), and future scope.

Two-Tier Audience System:

Explorers (Ages 8–13): Focuses on discovering natural aptitudes and interests to give broad developmental directions (e.g., coding, arts, logic). Hides financial/market constraints.

Navigators (Ages 14+): Merges teens and adults into one logical group. Delivers concrete, market-ready decisions for choosing streams, college degrees, or career pivots.

2. The "Data-Driven Ikigai" Engine
The assessment algorithm replaces vague philosophy with a strict mathematical model combining three scientific frameworks:

Passion (RIASEC / Holland Codes): Measures core interests across 6 traits (Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, Conventional).

Skill (Aptitude Tests): Measures cognitive strengths via timed micro-quizzes (Numerical, Logical, Spatial, Verbal).

Reality Check (Ikigai Integration): For the 14+ tier, user-adjusted sliders for salary goals, work-life balance, and market demand filter the database to find the perfect practical matches.

3. The Dynamic Item Bank
To guarantee test-retest reliability without boring the user, the app uses a dynamic, randomized question pool.

Session Size: 33 data points per test (18 RIASEC + 12 Aptitude + 3 Reality Sliders) to keep the test under 12 minutes.

Question Clusters: Instead of static questions, the database holds a "cluster" of variants (Action focus, Scenario focus) measuring the same psychological trait.

Total Initial Scope: 180 questions (90 for Explorers, 90 for Navigators) allowing users to retake the test multiple times with fresh prompts while yielding stable results.
